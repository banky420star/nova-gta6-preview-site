PURCHASE POLICIES

Checkout and availability

NOVA lists genuine game editions. A purchase is only possible once a product has an active Shopify checkout link, an approved price and available inventory. The payment page will display the final amount and order terms before the customer confirms payment.

Digital game codes

Where supplied through an authorized distribution channel, a digital game key will be delivered through the method specified on the product page. Codes can have console and regional restrictions. Do not share platform account passwords or verification codes.

Physical code-in-box delivery

If the physical product format is listed and available, a retail box containing a digital code will be shipped to the address entered at checkout. This does not mean a game disc is included. Dispatch timelines and shipping costs must be confirmed in the checkout.

Returns, cancellations and refunds

Customer cancellation or refund rights depend on the product format, whether a code has been issued or redeemed, supplier terms and applicable South African law. Refund queries for a NOVA purchase should reference the actual Shopify order. Purchases made directly through Sony or Microsoft must be handled by those platforms.

Business and contact details

Merchant business registration, customer-support contact information, VAT/tax status and contractual sales terms require verification in Shopify before customer sales are activated.
