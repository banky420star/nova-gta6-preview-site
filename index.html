08 — FAQs

THE DETAILS.

Is this the official Rockstar Games store?

No. NOVA GAME CO. is presented as an independent retailer. Rockstar is the game developer and publisher. Official art and trailers are linked from Rockstar or authorized platform storefronts.

When does GTA VI release in South Africa?

PlayStation confirms a midnight South African release on 19 November 2026.

Is there a disc inside the retail box?

No. Rockstar's physical code-in-box edition contains a digital download code rather than a game disc.

Is the physical box in limited supply?

Availability may be limited. Exact stock allocation and shipping dates require supplier confirmation; this demo does not reserve inventory.

Why does checkout ask for an email?

So a retailer can deliver a valid digital code where it has the rights and inventory to do so. Your PlayStation Online ID or Xbox gamertag may be used as an optional reference, but not for redeeming the game on your behalf.

Can I buy it from NOVA now?

Not through this demo. A real merchant account, legally sourced stock, business contact details and a verified payment gateway must be connected before NOVA accepts orders.
