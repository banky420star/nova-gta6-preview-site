Is NOVA the official Rockstar Games store?+

No. NOVA GAME CO. is an independent retail concept. Rockstar Games develops and publishes Grand Theft Auto VI. NOVA is not operated, sponsored or endorsed by Rockstar.

How do I purchase GTA VI from NOVA?+

Select a platform, edition and fulfillment format. Purchases are completed only through Shopify when the corresponding product is published, in stock and available in your region.

Which consoles will GTA VI support?+

The announced versions are PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. A PC version is not offered here.

What is the difference between Standard and Ultimate?+

Standard includes the full game. Ultimate adds a collection of special in-game items and upgrades. The exact benefits are published in Rockstar's official edition information.

What exactly is a code-in-box?+

It is a physical retail package containing a redeemable download code rather than a disc. A real code requires authentic supplier inventory and must match the supported region and platform.

Can digital codes be delivered by email?+

Digital download codes can be delivered only when they are available from an approved distribution channel. Confirm the supported region and delivery terms at Shopify checkout.

Does my PlayStation or Xbox account region matter?+

Yes. PlayStation voucher redemption is generally region-specific. Always verify platform, account country and code terms before purchase.

Are the prices and bonuses guaranteed?+

No. Prices shown here are illustrative platform references, not a live NOVA offer. Pre-order benefits vary by purchase date, retailer, format, territory and platform terms.

Where can I get official game support?+

Visit Rockstar Support for official product and technical support. Store-specific contacts and policies must be finalized before NOVA opens for sales.
