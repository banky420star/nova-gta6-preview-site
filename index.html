THE DETAILS.

Is this the official Rockstar Games store?

No. NOVA GAME CO. is presented as an independent retailer. Rockstar is the game developer and publisher. Official art and trailers are linked from Rockstar or authorized platform storefronts.

When does GTA VI release in South Africa?

PlayStation confirms a midnight South African release on 19 November 2026.

Is there a disc inside the retail box?

No. Rockstar's physical code-in-box edition contains a digital download code rather than a game disc.

Is the physical box in limited supply?

Availability may be limited. Exact stock allocation and shipping dates require supplier confirmation; no allocation is guaranteed until an order is confirmed.

How is a digital game delivered?

If NOVA stocks an authorized digital code, it is sent to the delivery email in the order. Redemption happens in your own PlayStation or Xbox account. Never share your console password or verification codes.

How do I buy GTA VI from NOVA?

Choose a console and edition above. A secure checkout link will be provided only when the relevant product is available in NOVA Shopify and its payment provider is active. Until then, purchasing is unavailable.
