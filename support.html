HELP WITH YOUR ORDER

Guidance for purchases made through NOVA, PlayStation and Xbox. NOVA cannot access your PlayStation or Microsoft account.

Payment & checkout

NOVA checkout is processed only through its own verified Shopify product link. Confirm your console, edition, payment amount and delivery format on the secure checkout page. The website will not ask for your console password.

See editions ↗

Purchase confirmation

After a genuine NOVA purchase, Shopify issues an order confirmation containing the order number and paid or pending payment status. A game code is only dispatched through verified fulfillment after successful payment.

Check purchasing status ↗

Refund requests for NOVA purchases

Contact NOVA using the contact details on your actual order confirmation. Refund eligibility depends on product type, supply agreement, redemption status and applicable South African consumer law. We do not claim Sony's refund decisions.

Read NOVA policies ↗

PlayStation Store refunds

For purchases made directly through PlayStation Store, follow Sony's official refund process. NOVA cannot refund a Sony-store transaction or manage your PSN billing.

Voucher code redemption

PlayStation voucher and game codes may be locked to a region. Ensure your console account country matches the code territory. Redeem on your own device or on the official platform website.
