DETAILS.

The order simulator is a client-side demonstration. It does not transmit, save, or process order-form entries through a server. No real orders are placed.

Data entered in the demo

Sample name, email address, platform choice, optional PSN Online ID or Xbox gamertag, region and optional shipping address are only used to render the sample receipt in the browser. Do not enter real addresses or other sensitive information unless needed for a local demonstration.

External media

Images displayed from Rockstar or platform-provider CDNs may cause a browser to make requests to those providers. Official YouTube videos are loaded only after you choose to play them. See the external providers' privacy notices for details.

When a live store launches

Before handling real customer data, the business will need a comprehensive privacy notice explaining the responsible party, data uses, service providers, retention, sharing and data-subject rights under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

Security

Never request or store a customer's PlayStation/Xbox password, two-factor authentication token, recovery code or payment card credentials on this site. A code-delivery email is sufficient to deliver a legitimately stocked digital code; the customer performs redemption directly with the platform.

Contact

Contact details for privacy requests must be added before commercial deployment. See Store Policies.
