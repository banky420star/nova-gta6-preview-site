NOVA GAME CO. / STORE INFORMATION

YOUR PRIVACY.

How the NOVA promotional site uses external media and how Shopify will handle customer transactions.

What the promotional website collects

This website is a static informational storefront. The game and edition selection is processed in your browser. The promotional site does not itself request payment-card data or account passwords. The selected product can be opened on the merchant Shopify website.

Shopify store and checkout

If you visit Shopify product pages, add items to cart, or purchase an available item, Shopify and the merchant process relevant order and contact information. The full merchant privacy notice must be finalized and published in Shopify before collecting real payments.

External media and embedded players

Rockstar and YouTube assets may cause your browser to connect to those providers. YouTube video playback is initiated only when you choose to open an official trailer. Those services apply their own privacy policies.

Customer rights

Customers may have rights under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) and other applicable law. The final merchant privacy notice must identify the responsible party, purposes, processors, retention rules, access procedures and how to exercise statutory rights.

Security

Do not enter PlayStation Network, Xbox or Rockstar account passwords, recovery codes or two-factor authentication codes anywhere on NOVA product pages. Payments are handled by Shopify checkout when available.

Data requests

Verified contact details for the responsible retailer and privacy requests must be added to Shopify before launch. For order-specific requests, use the contact information in the actual Shopify purchase confirmation.
