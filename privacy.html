PRIVACY & DATA

Visiting the site

This website presents game information and product-selection pages. It does not directly collect card numbers, PlayStation or Microsoft passwords, or account verification codes. No purchase is completed inside these static pages.

Shopify checkout

If and when NOVA enables payment, checkout and order information will be processed by Shopify and the configured payment provider. Customers should review the privacy notice shown at the actual checkout and the merchant's final published policy.

External content

The site references official game imagery from Rockstar Games content delivery sources, YouTube videos and official platform support links. Opening external services may involve cookies and technical data collection controlled by those providers.

Customer inquiries and rights
